Health-related data

Body weight, height, age, gender and training records may qualify as health-related data under Article 9 GDPR. We process this data only on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you enter it, and solely to provide the App's features. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the relevant entries or your entire account.

We do not collect: location data, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, device identifiers for advertising, or your browsing activity outside the App.

2. Legal basis for processing

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — account data and training data are required to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) — body and health-related data.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where we must retain records to comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising purposes. Data is shared only with the following processors and only to the extent necessary to run the App:

5. International transfers

Your data is stored in the European Union. Where Google processes data outside the EU as part of its infrastructure, it does so under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. For users in Türkiye, storage in the EU constitutes a transfer abroad under KVKK; see the Turkish version for the KVKK-specific disclosure.

6. How long we keep data

  • While your account exists — account, profile and training data are retained so the App can show your history.
  • On account deletion — your profile, exercises, workout logs, friend requests and friend-list entries are deleted, and your authentication record is removed. Deletion is immediate and is initiated from within the App (Profile → Delete Account).
  • Backups — residual copies may persist in our provider's backups for a short period before being overwritten.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Many of these you can exercise directly in the App: edit your profile to rectify data, and use Profile → Delete Account to erase everything. For anything else, write to [CONTACT E-MAIL]. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Access to stored data is enforced by server-side security rules: your workout logs are readable only by your own authenticated account, and no user can write to another user's records. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. In the EEA and the UK, users under 16 need the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact [CONTACT E-MAIL] and we will delete it.
