Method 1 — From within the app (instant)

If you still have the app on your phone, this is the fastest way:

  • Open the FamilyPulse app
  • Go to the Profile tab
  • Tap "Delete My Account and Data" at the bottom of the page
  • Confirm by entering your password

Deletion happens immediately.

Method 2 — By email request

If you've uninstalled the app or can no longer access your account, send an email from the email address registered to your account to [SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE] with the subject "Account deletion request".

Your request must come from the email address registered to your account so we can verify your identity. Requests are completed within 30 days at the latest.

Data that gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your account and profile information (name, email, age, weight, gender)
  • Your location history
  • Your weekly safety reports
  • Your family group membership

Data that is not deleted

  • If your family group has other remaining members, the group itself (its name, invite code, other members, and their data) is not deleted. If you were the last member of the group, the group is deleted as well.
  • Notification records previously sent to your family may remain in the group's alert history.

Retention period

Once deletion is complete, your data is removed from our systems. Location history is already automatically deleted after 14 days regardless.
