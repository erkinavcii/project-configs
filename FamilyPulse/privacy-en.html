3. Who We Share Data With

  • Members of your family group: your location, battery and ringer status, and any alerts generated.
  • Google (Firebase): data is stored and processed on Google Firebase infrastructure (authentication, database).
  • OpenStreetMap (map imagery): maps are fetched from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers. When a map is displayed, the tile coordinates of the area being viewed are sent to those servers along with your device's IP address. In technical terms this means the approximate area you are looking at becomes known to them. Your stored location records, your account, and your family group information are not sent to OpenStreetMap.
  • No sale of data: your data is never sold, rented, or transferred for marketing purposes to data brokers or any third party. It is shared only with the technical sub-processors needed to deliver the service; those listed above are the complete set.
  • Advertising: the current version displays no advertising and shares no data for advertising purposes. Should an ad-supported, sponsored, or differently tiered model be introduced in future, this policy will be updated and the change announced to users in advance.
  • Analytics and crash reporting: the current version contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and collects no data for those purposes. This policy will be updated if that changes.

4. International Data Transfers

For the service to function, the following data is transferred to servers outside Türkiye:

  • To Google Firebase: your account details, location data, device status (battery/ringer), alerts, and weekly reports.
  • To OpenStreetMap: your device's IP address and the tile coordinates of the area being viewed, whenever map imagery is requested (see Section 3).

5. Retention Periods

  • Location history: automatically deleted after 14 days.
  • Live location: only the latest value is kept; each update replaces the previous one.
  • Weekly reports and alerts: retained until your account is deleted.
  • Account information: retained until your account is deleted.

6. Your Rights

  • Deletion: from within the app (Profile → Delete My Account and Data) or via this page.
  • Stopping location sharing: any time, from the Profile section.
  • Health data: not collected at all in this version. When smartwatch integration launches, you will be asked for separate, explicit consent; declining will not affect any other feature of the app.
  • For your other rights (access, rectification, objection, etc.) under KVKK Art. 11, or GDPR Art. 15–21 if you are in the EEA — see also the EEA rights section of our Terms of Service — contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE].

8. Medical Disclaimer

FamilyPulse is not a medical device. No health data is processed in this version; any future heart-rate measurements and alerts will likewise not be usable for diagnosis, treatment, or medical decision-making — they are intended solely to inform family members.
