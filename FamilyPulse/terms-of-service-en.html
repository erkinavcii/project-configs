2. Description of the Service

FamilyPulse is a mobile application designed to let family members keep track of one another's safety. The current version offers:

  • Location sharing: While location sharing is enabled (including while the app runs in the background), family group members can see one another's live location on a map.
  • Safe zones (geofencing): You can define zones such as home or school; the rest of the family is notified when a member enters or leaves one.
  • Remote audio unmute: Family members can send a remote request to a silenced or do-not-disturb device. When the request goes through, the device's ringer mode is restored to normal, the ring, notification, and system volumes are raised to their maximum, and a notification sound is played. The feature only works if the device owner has granted Android's "Do Not Disturb access" permission, and the owner can switch it off at any time from Profile.
  • Weekly safety report: An automatically generated weekly summary of a family member's location, battery, and alert activity.
  • Family group management: Creating or joining a group with an invite code, and member management (see Section 5).

A future release may add an optional heart-rate/health anomaly monitoring feature via smartwatch integration, subject to a separate, explicit opt-in consent; no health data is collected or processed in the current version (see Section 10).

The Service is provided "as is" and "as available"; features may change, be improved, or be removed over time. The App is not a medical device or an emergency service (see Sections 9 and 10).

5. Family Group Rules

Family groups are subject to the following rules, reflecting how the App actually operates:

  • Member limit: A family group may have at most 5 members. This limit is the same for free and Premium groups.
  • Joining: New members can only join a group with a valid invite code.
  • Data sharing within a group is automatic: once you join a group, your location, battery/ringer status, safe-zone entries/exits, and weekly report are automatically shared with every current and future member of that group. You can stop this only by turning off location sharing (from Profile) or by leaving the group.
  • Admin role: the person who creates a group becomes its admin. If the admin leaves, the admin role automatically transfers to the next-longest-standing member.
  • Group dissolution: once the last remaining member leaves, the group and its invite code are permanently deleted.
  • Premium scope: Premium status applies at the family-group level, not per individual (see Section 7).
  • Responsibility for the invite code: an invite code lets anyone who knows it join the group. Keeping the code confidential, and verifying that whoever joins is actually the intended person, is entirely the responsibility of the user who shares it. The Provider is not liable for the exposure of group members' location and device data resulting from a code being shared with the wrong or an unauthorized person, or otherwise falling into third-party hands. Should this happen, you can end that access by leaving the group or creating a new one.
